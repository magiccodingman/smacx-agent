# PR 48 H1–H5 repair acceptance

## H4 — canonical orphan recovery

The journal validates the canonical chain under the existing root/timeline locks before returning an idempotent write, allocating a sequence, or replaying after restart. A unique contiguous valid suffix advances the manifest; corrupt hashes, scope mismatches, gaps, and competing suffixes fail explicitly without changing the manifest. Markers and indexes remain disposable. The unchanged own-writer fast path avoids rescanning prior payloads.

Acceptance: `journal_internal_crash_test.py` covers event-before-manifest with and without an idempotency key, manifest-before-marker, marker-before-catalog, S+2 linkage, and four invalid suffix adversaries. `campaign_journal_test.py` and `journal_idempotency_index_test.py` pass (zero canonical event reads for 100 new-key appends). `publication_transaction_test.py` passes 22 cases, including two failures inside real journal persistence followed by collector/store/journal recreation. Evidence is deterministic real-file journal persistence and native-shaped production collection, not running-game execution.

H1, H2/H3, H5 and integrated active-scope tail acceptance remain pending.
